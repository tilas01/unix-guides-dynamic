WHY THIS LICENCE
----------------

Information about how to install and harden your own computer should be free
to read and free to pass on, and this project is licensed so that it stays
that way.

The two conditions beyond attribution are there for one reason: this took a
long time to write, and it was written to be given away. Reposting it under
someone else's name, or putting it behind a paywall, takes something that was
free and makes it less free. That is the only outcome the licence is trying to
prevent.

So: a request, plainly. Please keep the credit on it, and please do not charge
people for it. Everything else — forking, editing, mirroring, teaching from it,
disagreeing with it in public — is welcome and always will be.
